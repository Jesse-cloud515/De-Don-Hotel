Privacy Policy

This notice explains how De Don Hospitality Limited ("DE DON", "we") collects and uses personal information through this website.

Demo text — to be reviewed by the hotel's legal adviser

Information we collect

When you request a reservation, send an enquiry or subscribe to our newsletter we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, stay dates, number of guests, country of residence and any requests you share with us.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, manage your reservation, communicate with you about your stay and, if you have subscribed, to send occasional news. We do not sell personal information.

Legal basis

We process reservation and enquiry data to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and newsletter data on the basis of your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

Retention

We keep reservation records for six years for tax and accounting purposes, and enquiry records for two years. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Sharing

Information is accessible only to authorised hotel staff and to service providers who help us operate this website and send email, under appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information by contacting privacy@dedonhotel.com.

Cookies

This website uses only cookies that are strictly necessary for the staff dashboard to function.
