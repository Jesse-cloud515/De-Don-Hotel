The House

More Than a Stay.
A Place to Belong.

DE DON was conceived as a residence first and a hotel second: a house where the pace slows the moment you arrive, where your preferences are remembered rather than requested, and where every detail is considered so that you never have to think about it.

Set on a quiet, tree-lined street in Maitama, we are minutes from the capital's diplomatic and business districts — yet a world away from them. Whether you are in Abuja for a summit, a celebration or simply to rest, our role is the same: to give you the quiet confidence of being looked after, beautifully.
