Our approach

Honest flavours,
careful service

Food is where hospitality becomes personal. Our kitchens, led by Executive Chef Adaeze Okafor, cook with respect for the ingredient and for the guest.

We buy from farmers across Nigeria — yams from Benue, vegetables from the Jos Plateau, fish from the Lagos coast — and pair them with classic and contemporary technique.

Restaurant

Ìtàn

"Ìtàn" means story in Yoruba. Our signature restaurant tells the story of Nigerian cuisine through a modern lens, with an open kitchen and a menu that changes with the seasons.
