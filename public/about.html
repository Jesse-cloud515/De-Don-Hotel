Our story

A house in
the capital

DE DON opened its doors in Maitama in 2024, founded by an Abuja family who had spent decades welcoming guests into their own home. Their idea was simple: build the hotel they wished existed in the capital — elegant without formality, private without isolation, and unmistakably Nigerian in its warmth.

Today a team of more than 200 people cares for our guests, many of whom return so often that they are greeted by name at the door. We measure ourselves not by stars or awards, but by how many of them feel they have come home.
